      1 · Add & edit locations

      • Add: press ＋ Add location on the map, then click the spot. Or right-click (long-press on phone) anywhere on the map.
      • Edit: press Edit next to any name in the list, or switch on ✎ Edit / move and tap a marker.
      • Move: with ✎ Edit / move on, drag a marker onto its building.
      Hollow markers with "?" are positions still to be verified.

      2 · Trace plant roads (for exact internal distances)

      Public road maps don't cover internal plant roads. Switch on tracing and click along each road on the satellite image. Click near an existing road to join it. Double-click or press Finish to end a road. Distances then follow your traced roads.

      3 · Save & share

      Edits are saved on this device only. To publish them, press Download data file and upload the saved locations.json to the server's data folder (replace the old file).
